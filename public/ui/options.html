ANCHOR / 1.0.0
YOUR BROWSER, WITH A HOME BASE.

A little more staying power.

Keep the places you return to. Let everything else open somewhere new.

Essentials

The master switch. Turn off to leave every tab alone.

Pin a tab in Chrome and Anchor takes care of its links.

Per-tab choices and workspace routing override this default.

Change an individual tab from the toolbar popup.

Explicit Ctrl/Cmd and middle-click behavior stays native.

A few exceptions.

A site rule changes how protected tabs behave; it does not protect an unpinned tab by itself. Rules match the exact protocol, host and port.

No wildcards. A subdomain is a different origin. Saving an existing origin replaces its rule.

Deliberate, not aggressive.

Useful for hash-routed apps. Also opens ordinary section links in new tabs.

Optional for search forms. Passwords, uploads and POST forms are never replayed.

After a direct address-bar or bookmark navigation, try opening the destination separately and reloading the previous page.

Recovery is not prevention. The page may already have unloaded and unsaved state may be lost. Redirects, script-only routing, Back/Forward and forms are excluded. Recovery disarms after one attempt per tab; “Use this page as home” re-arms it.

Assign optional commands at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Right-click a link to open it here and pause protection for five minutes.

Yours, and only yours.

Preferences and exact-origin rules live in this browser profile. No sync service, server, tracking code or browsing-history database.

Exports include site origins, not open tab URLs. Manual protection, pauses and saved home URLs are session-only; they reset on browser restart or extension reload. Pinned tabs are protected again automatically.

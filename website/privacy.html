Privacy, without the fine print.

Your browsing belongs to you.

Prepared for Matt Richmond · 3 October 2026 · Anchor 2.0.

No Anchor server.

No accounts, advertising, analytics SDK, telemetry endpoint or remote runtime code. Anchor processes data locally and does not send browsing information to its developer.

What Anchor reads.

Tab pin state, eligible link addresses and page/frame navigation context support link protection. Workspaces show supported open-page titles and addresses for your selection. Optional simple GET protection uses non-password/non-file fields for that browser request only. POST bodies are not intercepted or replayed; cookie and password stores are not read.

What stays on this device.

Local extension storage holds preferences, site rules and saved workspace names, labels, home URLs, protection/destination preferences and display selection. Temporary session storage holds live tab/window/document bindings, protection/navigation context, bounded gesture/source references, reservations, pending operations and layout readback. No passwords, POST bodies or captured screen images are stored.

Optional display access.

The optional system.display permission reads monitor names, IDs, sizes and usable work areas to arrange windows on your chosen display. It is not screenshot capture or screen recording.

Exports and other services.

Exports are local files you request; saved home URLs may contain private paths or tokens. Review before sharing. Websites opened in the browser receive normal requests and use their own policies. Chrome/OS services and user-configured browser behavior are outside Anchor's control. The supplied website has no tracking code; a future host may keep access logs. Support email uses normal email services.

Your controls.

Delete workspaces or reset local data from Settings; pause/release without closing pages. Removing the extension removes its browser-managed storage, not exported files or Chrome's normal history. Incognito is disabled. The publisher must confirm and host this policy at a real URL before store submission.
